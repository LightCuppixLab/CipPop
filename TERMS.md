## =============== 2. The App ===============

---

Cip Pop is a collection of paper games for children aged 4 to 7: drums, a tower of blocks, drawing, a meadow walk, a world to build, a tiny planet, a bear's wood, a rocket trip to the stars, a dinosaur dig, a secret lake, a dragon flight, a world of wonders and a cat among the clouds. Games, sounds, voice and pictures are included in the App and work offline.

No account is required. The App contains no advertising and no in-app purchases. Any download price is shown by the App Store before you confirm. Supported devices, languages and operating-system requirements are shown in the App Store listing.

Cip Pop is designed for young children. A parent or guardian should choose whether the App is suitable for their child, accept the App Store terms on the child's behalf where needed, and supervise its use. Apple's parental controls and Screen Time can help manage access and time spent.

The settings for parents (voice, sounds, vibrations, voice choice and the link to the language settings) are behind a check that asks for an adult's year of birth. This check helps keep children out of the settings; it is not an identity check.

## =============== 4. Drawings and game progress ===============

---

Each game starts fresh. The state of a game is kept only while it is open, and drawings made in the Drawing game are deleted when the child leaves it. The App does not provide backups or synchronization between devices, so nothing made in the App should be relied on as a permanent record.
