# Privacy Policy for Cip Pop

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This policy covers Cip Pop on iPhone and iPad. Cip Pop is a collection of paper games for children aged 4 to 7, designed to be used offline and without any account.

## 1. Who is responsible

Cip Pop is provided by Light Cup Pix Lab, Padova, Italy.

Privacy contact: **lightcuppixlab.dev@gmail.com**.

## 2. In short

- Cip Pop does not collect, send or share any personal information.
- It has no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases.
- It does not connect to the internet: all games, sounds and pictures are inside the App.
- It does not ask for access to the camera, microphone, photos, contacts or location.
- The little information it keeps stays on the device, as described below.

## 3. Information kept on the device

To work, the App keeps a few pieces of information only on the device, in its own private storage:

- **Parents' settings**: whether the voice, sounds and vibrations are on, and the system voice chosen in the parents' area.
- **The game in progress**: while a game is open, its current state is saved so it can resume if the screen is reloaded. It is deleted when the child leaves the game and every time the App starts.
- **Drawings**: pictures made in the Drawing game are saved as images in the App's protected storage, so they can be hung on the paper fridge. They are deleted when the child leaves the Drawing game and every time the App starts.

This information is never sent to us or to anyone else. We cannot see it, access it or delete it remotely. Device backups or transfers managed by Apple may include local app data, depending on the device settings.

## 4. Parents' area

The settings for parents open only after typing the year of birth of an adult. The year is used just once, to check that the person is an adult, and is never saved or sent anywhere.

From the parents' area, the "Change" button next to Language opens the iOS Settings app, where a different language can be chosen for Cip Pop. This is the only place where the App leaves its own screens, and it is behind the parents' check.

## 5. Voice

The words and names spoken in the games are read aloud by Apple's speech system, using the voices installed on the device. The text is created by the App itself and is processed on the device. Cip Pop does not record or listen to anything: it never uses the microphone.

Extra natural voices can be downloaded by a parent from the iOS Settings app; that download is handled by Apple under its own terms.

## 6. Advertising, tracking and third parties

Cip Pop does not contain third-party advertising, advertising identifiers, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, social features or links to websites. No information is shared with third parties.

Apple may separately process App Store information and diagnostics according to the device settings and [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). This is distinct from the App's own behaviour.

## 7. Children

Cip Pop is made for young children and is designed so that children never have to give any personal information. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children or from anyone else.

If you believe a child has sent personal information to our support address, please contact us and we will delete it.

## 8. Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address, your message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them only to answer your request and to fix the reported issue. Please send only what is needed; do not send information about your child.

For support correspondence, the relevant GDPR bases are taking steps at your request and our legitimate interest in answering questions and maintaining the App. We keep correspondence only while it is needed to handle your request and any related legal obligations. We do not use support messages for advertising and we do not sell them. Our email provider processes messages to deliver and store them.

## 9. Control, retention and deletion

Settings can be changed at any time in the parents' area. Game states and drawings are removed automatically, as described above. Deleting the App together with its data removes everything it kept on the device. Backups kept by Apple must be managed through the device or Apple settings.

## 10. Your privacy rights

Where applicable, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of personal information we hold, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Since the App does not collect personal information, these rights mainly concern support emails. Contact the address above; we may need proportionate information to verify your request.

You may also complain to your local data protection authority, including the Italian Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.

## 11. Security and updates

Drawings are stored with the device's data protection. Keep the device and its operating system up to date, as local data depends on device security. No system can guarantee absolute security.

If the App's data practices change, we will update this policy and its date. Where legally required, we will provide notice or obtain consent before introducing any new processing.

For privacy questions: **lightcuppixlab.dev@gmail.com**.
